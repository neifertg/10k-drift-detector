How a passage is compared, and what this does not do

How a change is called

Passages are split on bold headings where the filing has them, otherwise on paragraphs. Passages are paired one-to-one, greedily, when content-token overlap is at least 0.45. A leftover whose closest passage is still at or above that line was not paired because the closest passage was already matched; it is labeled already paired, not added or removed. Overlap from 0.30 up to, but not including, 0.45 is left unclassified. Below 0.30, the passage is added or removed.

Content tokens drop function words and keep negations, so removing “not” is not treated as noise. A matched pair is date-only when every added or removed token is a four-digit year, a month name, or an integer from 1 to 31 sitting within three tokens of a month name. Other number changes are kept.

“Material weakness” and “significant deficiency” are classified from the sentence. A sentence that says a weakness was identified is labeled assertion. A sentence that only says one may occur in the future is labeled hypothetical. The auditor’s standard sentence about assessing the risk that a material weakness exists is labeled a procedure description. None of those is reported as an identification. If the rules do not fit, the sentence is quoted and no classification is made.

The auditor's opinion is taken only from words in Item 9A. A sentence that points to Item 8 is quoted as a pointer. Item 8 is not opened.

Peer “not aligned” means no passage in the peer's extracted Item 1A for the two years in that column cleared the overlap floor. It does not mean the peer has no such risk.

What this does not do

  • It does not score the company, and it does not decide whether a control is effective.
  • It does not read Item 8, exhibits, 10-Q, 8-K, or the 10-K/A.
  • It does not put peers on one fiscal calendar. The peer page states the period ends.
  • Passage matching is greedy and one-to-one. A leftover can overlap another passage at or above 0.45 when that passage was already paired. A full rewrite can show up as a removal plus an addition.
  • An integer from 1 to 31 next to a month name is treated as a date.
  • The optional model summary is off unless you set it, and it is never the evidence.
  • The short list is drafted from the extracted changes. It is published only after each quote is found, after whitespace normalization, in the cited Item 1A or Item 9A extract, and in a stored passage or Item 9A quote for that accession. The audit-plan line is a consideration, not a finding.
  • This is a wording comparison of public filings. It is not investment advice.
